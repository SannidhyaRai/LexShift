How to read it. *Postings scored* and *judgments scored* are the machine-independent work; the time also includes building A. *overlap@10* is the share of exact BM25's top 10 that the method keeps. nDCG@10 uses the dev qrels, which were pooled from exact systems: a method that surfaces judgments nobody judged is scored as if they were irrelevant (see judged@10), so its nDCG change is a pessimistic estimate.
